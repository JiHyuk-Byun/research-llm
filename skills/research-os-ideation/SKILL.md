# Research-OS Ideation

Read `runs/{run_id}/wiki/index.md`, relevant run-local synthesis pages, followups, and source notes before ideating.

## Rules

- Ground ideas in known gaps, contradictions, or bottlenecks.
- Mark speculative ideas clearly.
- Prefer ideas with testable hypotheses.
- Save durable idea notes under `runs/{run_id}/wiki/outputs/` when assigned.
- Request knowledge-building if the wiki lacks enough evidence.
