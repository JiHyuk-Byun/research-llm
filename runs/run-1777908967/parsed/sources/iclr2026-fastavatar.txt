Abstract:
FastAvatar is a feed-forward 3D avatar reconstruction framework that takes inputs such as a single image, multi-view observations, or monocular video and reconstructs a 3D Gaussian Splatting avatar model within seconds. It uses a Large Gaussian Reconstruction Transformer with multi-frame aggregation, camera/expression/head-pose guidance, and incremental Gaussian aggregation.

Relevant extracted technical details:
- The paper emphasizes variable-length input handling because real-world avatar observations can include arbitrary numbers of frames.
- FastAvatar can incrementally use additional observations to improve reconstruction quality.
- Its Large Gaussian Reconstruction Transformer aggregates and registers face tokens, injects 3D positional prompts, and predicts canonical 3DGS attributes.
- The method includes landmark tracking loss and sliced fusion loss to support alignment and incremental fusion.
- The method prunes redundant 3D Gaussian primitives because primitive count can grow linearly with input frame count in incremental scenarios.
- The reported domain is avatar/head reconstruction, so it is supporting rather than primary evidence for general online scene point-map reconstruction.
