StreamSplat is an ICLR 2026 Poster about online dynamic 3D reconstruction from uncalibrated video streams. The OpenReview metadata describes a fully feed-forward framework that transforms arbitrary-length uncalibrated video streams into dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting representations online. The stated motivation is that dynamic reconstruction methods often rely on per-scene optimization and full-sequence access, which creates latency and memory constraints.

Technical elements reported in the OpenReview abstract:
- Probabilistic sampling to predict 3D Gaussians from uncalibrated inputs.
- A bidirectional deformation field for frame associations and lower long-term error accumulation.
- Adaptive Gaussian fusion that propagates persistent Gaussians while handling emerging and vanishing ones.
- Experiments on dynamic and static benchmarks, with a reported 1200x speedup over optimization-based methods.
