Hyden is an ICLR 2026 Poster about high-resolution monocular geometry estimation. The OpenReview abstract describes a hybrid dual-path encoder for monocular depth, point-map, and surface-normal estimation. The architecture combines a low-resolution Vision Transformer branch for global context with a full-resolution CNN branch for fine detail, and fuses features with a lightweight MLP before decoding.

Technical elements reported in the OpenReview abstract:
- The design constrains transformer computation to fixed resolution while using CNN scaling for high-resolution inputs.
- The paper introduces a self-distillation framework using pseudo-labels from existing models at both lower-resolution full-image and high-resolution crop levels.
- The approach is integrated into DepthAnything-v2 for depth estimation and MoGe2 for surface-normal and metric point-map prediction.
- The reported relevance to this run is efficient point-map/depth estimation, not online streaming reconstruction.
