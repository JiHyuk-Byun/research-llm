# MoRGS: Efficient Per-Gaussian Motion Reasoning for Streamable Dynamic 3D Scenes

## Main Claims

- MoRGS targets online reconstruction of dynamic scenes from streaming multi-view inputs under low-latency constraints.
- The method treats per-Gaussian motion as a core modeling object for dynamic 3D scenes.
- The source claims state-of-the-art reconstruction quality and motion fidelity among online methods while maintaining streamable performance.

## Evidence and Locations

- Abstract: frames online dynamic-scene reconstruction as learning from streaming multi-view inputs under low latency.
- Abstract: says existing online approaches can be efficient and visually strong but may optimize motion according to photometric residuals rather than true 3D motion.
- Abstract: describes sparse optical-flow cues, a per-Gaussian motion offset field, and per-Gaussian motion confidence.

## Methods / Approach

- Uses optical flow from a sparse set of key views as a lightweight motion cue.
- Learns a per-Gaussian motion offset field to reconcile projected 3D motion with observed flow across views and time.
- Introduces per-Gaussian motion confidence to distinguish dynamic and static Gaussians and weight Gaussian attribute residual updates.
