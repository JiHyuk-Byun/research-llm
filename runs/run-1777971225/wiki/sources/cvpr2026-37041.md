# MotionCrafter: Repurposing Video Generators for Dense Geometry and Motion Reconstruction

## Main Claims

- MotionCrafter is presented as the first video diffusion-based framework that jointly reconstructs 4D geometry and estimates dense motion from monocular video.
- The method represents point maps and scene flows in a unified latent space compatible with pretrained video generators.
- The source claims state-of-the-art joint 4D geometry reconstruction and dense scene-flow estimation without post-optimization.

## Evidence and Locations

- Abstract: states that MotionCrafter jointly reconstructs 4D geometry and dense motion from monocular video.
- Abstract: describes a 4D VAE that encodes point maps and scene flows as a unified latent.
- Abstract: reports 38.64% and 25.0% improvements in geometry and motion reconstruction, respectively.

## Methods / Approach

- Repurposes pretrained video generators for geometry and motion reconstruction.
- Trains a 4D VAE for point maps and scene flows.
- Uses a data normalization and VAE training strategy intended to improve transfer of diffusion priors.
