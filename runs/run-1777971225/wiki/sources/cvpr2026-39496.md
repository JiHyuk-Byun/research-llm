# MoVieS: Motion-Aware 4D Dynamic View Synthesis in One Second

## Main Claims

- MoVieS reconstructs 4D dynamic scenes from monocular videos in one second.
- The method uses pixel-aligned Gaussian primitives with explicitly supervised time-varying motions.
- The source claims unified modeling of appearance, geometry, and motion for reconstruction, view synthesis, and 3D point tracking.

## Evidence and Locations

- Abstract: states that MoVieS reconstructs 4D dynamic scenes from monocular videos in one second.
- Abstract: describes pixel-aligned Gaussian primitives and explicit supervision of time-varying motions.
- Abstract: says the framework supports zero-shot scene-flow estimation and moving-object segmentation.

## Methods / Approach

- Represents dynamic 3D scenes with pixel-aligned Gaussian primitives.
- Supervises time-varying motions explicitly.
- Bridges view synthesis with geometry reconstruction to allow large-scale training on diverse datasets.
